## Notes

- The films appear only as short excerpts shown playing inside Vestra
  Player, never as the film on its own:
  - Big Buck Bunny (the 60 fps 2160p "sunflower" render,
    `bbb_sunflower_2160p_60fps_normal.mp4`, in the playlist recording under
    the display name `Big Buck Bunny (2008).mp4`): the hero centre clip
    (`adjust`), the adjustments, playlist, store and AI recordings (the feature
    tour plays `adjust-short`, `playlist-short`, `store-4` and `ai-5`) and
    their posters, the window frame stills, and the blurred back-row stills.
    Recoloured on screen (the Picture panel turns it grey and back; Colour
    Wheels grade it in the AI clip `ai-5`), cropped to the player window or shown
    fullscreen, scaled and cut; sped up in places (the adjustments and store
    recordings).
  - Spring: the last seconds of the Plugin Store recording (`store-4`), the
    official Blender Studio upload played through VestraTube, the first
    seconds of the film. Zoomed and cropped with the recording, which is cut
    and sped up in places; not recoloured.
  - Elephants Dream, Sintel, Tears of Steel and Caminandes 2: in the playlist
    recording (`playlist-short`) as playlist rows, each with a small thumbnail
    (one frame near the start of the film, made by the player) and its
    duration. Sintel is double-clicked and the first seconds of the film play
    for about 2 s (scaled with the recording, not recoloured); the others do
    not play. The files are the official downloads from
    download.blender.org (see `C:\vestra\testmedia\blender-open-movies\CREDITS.md`
    outside the repository for the exact files and digests).
- Licence sources (checked 2026-11-04): Big Buck Bunny,
  https://peach.blender.org/about/ (CC BY 3.0; for reused parts of the movie
  the credit "(c) copyright 2008, Blender Foundation / www.bigbuckbunny.org").
  Spring, https://studio.blender.org/films/spring/pages/about/ (CC BY 4.0;
  the credit "© Blender Foundation | cloud.blender.org/spring").
- Vestra Player is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Blender Foundation.
  The footer says so under the credit lines, with the excerpt note, in both
  languages (`ft.creditsNote` in `src/i18n.json`).
- Twin Dragons is the open-source NES homebrew by Garydos (music and design),
  surt (art) and Shiru (SFX), as its title screen credits them: code under the
  zlib licence (https://github.com/Garydos/twin-dragons-nes), graphics under
  CC BY 3.0 (https://opengameart.org/content/twin-dragons). It is not the
  commercial game of the same name by Broke Studio, and Vestra Player is not
  affiliated with its authors. The VestraNES recording (`nes-3`; a still of

  player window and scaled; the ROM itself is not distributed with the page.
