## Notes

- The films appear only as short excerpts, shown playing inside Vestra
  Player or, in the explainers of 2026-11-12 (motion graphics that explain
  what a plugin does, rendered by the promo video's pipeline), as single
  frames and a short loop beside diagrams; never as the film on its own:
  - Big Buck Bunny (the 60 fps 2160p "sunflower" render,
    `bbb_sunflower_2160p_60fps_normal.mp4`, in the playlist recording under
    the display name `Big Buck Bunny (2008).mp4`): the hero centre clip
    (`adjust`), the player 0.8.0 recordings of 2026-11-12 (Picture, Sound,
    Seek preview, Playlist, Command Palette; the full clips `picture-9`,
    `palette-9`, `playlist-9` and the theme loop `theme-player`), Ask Vestra (a 480p copy, `bbb-480p-web.mp4`: `theme-ai` and the full clip
    `ask-9`), the AI clip `ai-5` and their posters, the window frame stills,
    and the blurred back-row stills.
    Recoloured on screen (the Picture panel turns it grey and back; Colour
    Wheels grade it in the AI clip `ai-5`), cropped to the player window or shown
    fullscreen, scaled and cut; sped up in places (the theme loops).
  - Elephants Dream, Sintel, Big Buck Bunny and Caminandes 2: in the playlist
    recording (`playlist-9`) as playlist rows, each with a small thumbnail
    (one frame near the start of the film, made by the player) and its
    duration. Sintel is double-clicked and the first seconds of the film play
    for about 2 s (scaled with the recording, not recoloured); the others do
    not play. The files are the official downloads from
    download.blender.org (see `C:\vestra\testmedia\blender-open-movies\CREDITS.md`
    outside the repository for the exact files and digests).
  - Sintel: the Clip Export explainer (five frames from 4:42 to 4:58 as a
    film strip, three of them lifted into a card that plays a 2.4 s loop from
    4:50 as the GIF), the Highlights explainer (the soundtrack's EBU R128
    short-term loudness over the first 12 minutes, measured with ffmpeg and
    drawn as a curve, and one frame at each of the peaks 1:06, 4:54, 5:38 and
    9:04, joined into a reel), the Clip Export full clip (`clipexport-9`: a
    12.8 s range exported as an animated GIF, the Collection, Highlights by
    loudness), and the playlist row that is double-clicked. Scaled and cut;
    not recoloured.
  - Caminandes 2: the Auto Frame explainer shows a copy made for it, the film
    at 2560 x 1440 padded into the top-left corner of a 3840 x 2160 green
    frame (`autoframe-caminandes2-4k-topleft`, its frame at 8.6 s), the
    picture scaled up as Auto Frame does, and the player's own result (R12);
    also a playlist row and a Clip Export Collection part.
  - Spring: the VestraTube search for "Spring Blender" shows about six result
    rows as YouTube returned them (Blender Studio's upload first, then other
    channels' titles and thumbnails: user ruling 2026-11-12, so that viewers
    see search results; it overrides D8), then the film's first seconds playing.
  - Big Buck Bunny, AI Upscaling comparison: the frame Ask Vestra's recording
    ends on (`bbb-480p-web.mp4` at 0:26.03, held), played in the player with AI
    Upscaling off and on and captured, shown whole and zoomed on a detail.
  - Sita Sings the Blues (Nina Paley, 2008; the visuals CC0 since 18 January
    2013, the old songs still under copyright and not used): one frame at 36:00
    of `Sita_Sings_the_Blues_small.mp4` (320 x 176) from
    https://archive.org/details/Sita_Sings_the_Blues, held and played in the
    player with Shader Upscale off and Anime4K A+A on, captured, shown whole and
    zoomed on a detail (`C:/vestra/testmedia/upscale-demo` outside the
    repository). No sound is used.
